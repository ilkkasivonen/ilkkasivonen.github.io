Caesar salad

On this page: Recipe details, Servings, Ingredients, and Steps.

A recipe for classic Caesar salad with homemade dressing. Crisp romaine lettuce, crunchy croutons and plenty of Parmesan, all brought together with a punchy anchovy dressing.

Prep time
15 minutes
Cooking time
None
Total time
15 minutes
Dietary
Not suitable for vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free diets
Allergens
  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Milk
  • Gluten
Equipment
Large mixing bowl, whisk, knife and cutting board, cheese grater

Change the number to set the serving size and recalculate the ingredients — currently 4 servings (original: 4).

Ingredients

Dressing

  • 1 garlic clove
  • 2 anchovy fillets
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Salad

  • 2 large romaine lettuce heads
  • 80 g Parmesan
  • 150 g croutons

Caesar salad

A recipe for classic Caesar salad with homemade dressing. Crisp romaine lettuce, crunchy croutons and plenty of Parmesan, all brought together with a punchy anchovy dressing.

Prep time
15 minutes
Cooking time

Ingredients

Dressing

  • 1 garlic clove
  • 2 anchovy fillets
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Salad

  • 2 large romaine lettuce heads
  • 80 g Parmesan
  • 150 g croutons

Equipment

  • Large mixing bowl
  • Whisk
  • Knife and cutting board
  • Cheese grater

Croissants

On this page: Recipe details, Servings, Ingredients, and Steps.

Buttery, flaky French croissants made from laminated dough. The dough is mixed, chilled, then folded around a slab of butter and rolled out repeatedly to create dozens of paper-thin layers. A weekend project that rewards patience with bakery-quality pastries.

Prep time
45 minutes
Cooking time
20 minutes
Total time
13 hours (includes overnight chilling — active time is about 1 hour)
Dietary
Not suitable for vegan, dairy-free, or gluten-free diets
Allergens
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Gluten
Equipment
Stand mixer with dough hook (or large bowl), rolling pin, ruler, sharp knife or pizza wheel, baking trays, parchment paper, pastry brush, plastic wrap

Change the number to set how many croissants the recipe makes — currently 12 (original: 12).

Ingredients

Dough

  • 500 g strong white bread flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 10 g fine salt
  • 80 g caster sugar
  • 10 g instant yeast
  • 300 ml cold whole milk
  • 40 g unsalted butter, softened

Lamination

  • 280 g cold unsalted butter, high-fat European-style if possible

Shaping & baking

  • 1 large egg (for egg wash)
  • 1 tbsp milk (for egg wash)

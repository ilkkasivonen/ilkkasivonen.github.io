Ramen

On this page: Recipe details, Servings, Ingredients, and Steps.

A weeknight ramen with a rich, savoury broth, soft-boiled eggs with jammy yolks, springy noodles and fresh toppings. The broth, eggs, noodles and toppings are prepared in parallel and brought together at the end, so timing matters more than in a stew or salad.

Prep time
15 minutes
Cooking time
30 minutes
Total time
45 minutes
Dietary
Not suitable for vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free diets
Allergens
  • Eggs
  • Gluten
  • Soy
Equipment
Large saucepan for broth, medium saucepan for eggs, second medium saucepan for noodles, slotted spoon, knife and cutting board, ladle, two deep serving bowls

Ingredients

Broth

  • 800 ml chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp miso paste
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, finely chopped

Soft-boiled eggs

  • 2 large eggs

Noodles

  • 200 g fresh ramen noodles, or 150 g dried

Toppings

  • 2 sheets of nori
  • 2 spring onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Chilli oil to taste (optional)

Timing plan

The four components — broth, eggs, noodles and toppings — overlap in time. A workable order is:

  1. Start the broth first so it has time to develop;
  2. Once the broth is simmering, start the eggs and prepare the toppings;
  3. Cook the noodles last, so they go straight from the pot into the bowl.
